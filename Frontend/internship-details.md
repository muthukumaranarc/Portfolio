### Overview

Selected for the **6-week Virtual IBM SkillsBuild Machine Learning and Applied AI Internship 2026**, offered in collaboration with **AICTE** and **BharatCares**.

The internship is designed to build foundational knowledge in **Machine Learning and Applied AI** through hands-on learning, guided mentorship, expert-led masterclasses, and real-world project development. The program covers AI model building and evaluation across supervised, unsupervised, deep, and reinforcement learning.
